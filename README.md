# Agentic AI Engineering

A practical learning, research and engineering journey exploring how to design, assemble, build, govern, evaluate, operate and improve agentic AI systems—and transfer validated capabilities across sectors.

## Practical operating method

Learn the relevant concepts, discover existing solutions, assemble suitable capabilities, test them, build only genuine gaps and assess the transfer of validated principles to other sectors.

- [Practical Build and Sector Transfer Method](docs/PRACTICAL_BUILD_AND_SECTOR_TRANSFER.md)

## Key project principles

- Capability before technology.
- Assemble before building.
- Reuse before repetition.
- Complexity only when justified.
- Evidence and verification.
- Continuity and portability.
- Document as we learn.

## Scope note

The curriculum is technology-neutral. Tools and technologies are investigated when a specific learning or design requirement makes them relevant, not simply because they are mentioned or advertised. Blockchain is not assumed to be a compulsory component of agentic AI.

Agriculture is the first practical pilot path. It is where we will investigate needs, assemble and test capabilities, and identify reusable principles. Real estate, mining, healthcare and other sectors are expansion domains for validated principles, subject to their own requirements and controls.
